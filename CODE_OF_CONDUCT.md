# Code of Conduct

## Our standard

AIX contributors and maintainers are expected to make participation respectful,
welcoming, and constructive. Be considerate in technical discussions, critique
ideas rather than people, respect differing experience levels, and accept
responsibility for mistakes.

Harassment, discrimination, threats, deliberate intimidation, sustained
disruption, publication of private information, and other conduct that would be
inappropriate in a professional setting are not acceptable.

## Scope

This policy applies in project repositories, issue trackers, pull requests,
discussions, and other spaces where someone represents the AIX project.

## Enforcement

Report conduct concerns privately to the repository maintainers through the
contact method listed on the maintainer's GitHub profile. Do not include API
keys, conversation contents, or other unrelated sensitive information.

Maintainers may edit or remove contributions and may temporarily or permanently
restrict participation when behavior violates this policy. Reports will be
reviewed promptly and handled as confidentially as practical.
